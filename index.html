01 · The Studio

We don't tie ourselves to a single industry. We follow the problems that genuinely deserve to be solved, and the people around us are as much a part of the work as the ideas themselves.

We are entrepreneurs, designers, makers and problem-solvers — a small team of genuinely curious minds.

Good Monkeys is a new-generation venture studio based in Dubai. We started as a handful of people who kept noticing the same thing: the most interesting opportunities rarely respect the borders between industries. So rather than commit to a single field, we chose to follow our curiosity wherever it happened to lead.

Today that curiosity takes many shapes — immersive science experiences, mobile apps for iOS and Android, applied artificial intelligence, a global trading network and the occasional adventure. As different as they look from the outside, every one of them begins in the same place and is held to the same standards of craft and care.
